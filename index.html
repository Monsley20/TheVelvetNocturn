FROM THE GARDEN · I

The Daffodil

A poem of death, devotion, and rebirth.

As the worms chew through my body
I decompose feeding nutrients to the filth
I feel what's left of my body losing feeling
Yet the ache doesn’t leave the body

With all strength I look up at the falling rain
For I see beauty
I see my soul dancing with hers.
She looks happy…

Which makes me happy one last time
before my eyes close forever

FROM THE GARDEN · II

The Earth Could Not Keep You

A promise made beyond the grave.

My hand tightens around the cold wood.
With quiet steps, I find you.

I drive the shovel into the earth.
I dig.
Tirelessly.

The dirt clings to you.
Jealous, I tear you from its grasp.

I made you a promise.
And I intend to keep it.

Still beautiful.
Still mine.

I kiss your lips
and lay beside you.

FROM THE GARDEN · III

A Weep Away

A room full of feelings left unspoken.

The only comfort I could find is my own solitude.
In the dark, cold, deafening silence of my own room.

They have dug their grave and they beg to come out
The tears
My emotions.

For once I truly feel what it's like.
To Weep

However like always, I wake, and remember.
I can't.

FROM THE GARDEN · IV

The Beautiful Prison

A love that became impossible to escape.

I can't tell you where I end,
Every thought ends with you.

I feel exhausted, trying to manifest what will never be.

This beautifully hand crafted prison I've created for myself has no escape.
As if I locked all exits and tossed the key.

I don't want to leave,
For I want only you
but your not in here, for you will never be.
